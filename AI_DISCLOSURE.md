# Use of AI

Furrow was built with substantial help from an AI coding assistant (Claude, by Anthropic). The assistant wrote most of the code and the interface text, working from the team's instructions.

The team (Valerii and Veta) chose the challenge and the approach, directed the design and the wording through many rounds of review, picked the example fields, and tested the tool. The team is responsible for the result.

The tool itself contains no generative AI. Its only machine-learning step is k-means clustering, used to group the 25 growing seasons into kinds of year.
